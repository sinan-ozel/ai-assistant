# **LYCANTHROPES IN EBERRON**



What if werewolves of Eberron were twelve
different “tribes”, each impacted by a different
moon of Eberron?
This book is inspired by the [homebrewed](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XxNb80pKTArS2SecLkFQE3nnbXTwt2j9/view) lunar
calendar of [Eberron](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XxNb80pKTArS2SecLkFQE3nnbXTwt2j9/view) by abexy and the classical
World of Darkness game, Werewolf: the
Apocalypse by White Wolf.
The rules are intended for players to play as
lycanthropes, specifically werewolves, in Eberron
campaigns.

## NEW RULES OF LYCANTROPY

In D&D 5e, lycanthropy is a curse or disease
that can be contracted through a bite or scratch
from a lycanthrope. In this version, lycantropy
comes in two forms: a **hereditary** form passed
down through bloodlines, and a **contracted**
form that is caught like a disease. If it is
hereditary, the character is born with
lycanthropy, and this cannot be removed, short
of a _wish_ spell.

##### HEREDITARY LYCANTHROPY


A character with either a parent with hereditary
lycantropy has a 50% chance of inheriting the
trait. If both parents have hereditary
lycanthropy, it is guaranteed. The probability
decreases to 25% (4 on a 1 _d_ 4) if only one
grandparent has hereditary lycanthropy, and
12.5% (8 on a 1 _d_ 8) if only one great-grandparent
has it, and so on. Overall, in the world of
Eberron, hereditary lycanthropy is rare but
persistent.
Werewolves, the most common type of
hereditary lycanthropes, have an affinity to one
of the twelve moons of Eberron, which influences
their lycanthropic form and abilities. Werewolves
of the same moon tend to form close-knit packs
or tribes, often centered around shared beliefs
and customs related to their moon.
Through parent to cub, werewolves pass down
not only their lycanthropic nature but also
knowledge of their moon’s influence, rituals, and



traditions. Each one of the moons correspond to
a tribe, and the traditions are detailed below.
Finally, werewolves with hereditary
lycanthropy have control over their
transformations, and are able to hold in-between
forms as well - five forms in total.

##### CONTRACTED LYCANTHROPY


A character bitten or scratched by a lycanthrope
must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw.
On a failed save, the character “is cursed” with
lycanthropy, as per the original rules.
They cannot pass this on to their offspring,
even if they become a lycanthrope themselves.
They can, however “infext” others through bites
and scratches, as per the original rules.
If they embrace their lycantropy, they can get
feats to control their transformations and gain
abilities based on the moon that influenced the
lycanthrope that infected them.
They gain all the benefits and drawbacks of
lycanthropy, including the five forms detailed
below.

## THE FORMS

A lycanthrope has five forms:

- **Humanoid** **Form:** The lycanthrope appears as
a normal humanoid of their race.

- **Shifter** **Form:** The lycanthrope takes on a mix
of humanoid and animal features, gaining









1


some of the abilities of their animal form. In
this form, they resemble Shifters.

- **Hybrid** **Form:** This is the hybrid form from
D%D 5e.

- **Dire** **Form:** The lycanthrope takes on a larger
and more powerful version of their animal
form, gaining enhanced abilities.

- **Animal** **Form:** The lycanthrope fully
transforms into their animal form.

Contracted lycanthropes have no control over
their transformations and will shift into their
Hybrid form during a full moon. However,
hereditary lycanthropes have control over their
shifts and can choose to shift into any of the five
forms.
To shift between these forms, the lycanthrope
must use an action on their turn. They can shift
only to the next form up or down the list as one
action. For example, they can shift from
Humanoid to Shifter, or from Hybrid to Dire, but
not Humanoied to Hybrid takes two turns.
This transformation, “shifting”, requires a
Wisdom check. If their moon is dark (new moon),
the check is made with disadvantage. If their
moon is full, the check is made with advantage.
The DC of the check is 18 - the character level in
the werewold class/2 (rounded down), as per the

characters will not be able to rely on their
shifting abilities as much, outside of the full
moon phase. To determine the phase of the

## INVOLUNTARY SHIFTING


Contracted lycanthropes must shift into their
Hybrid form during a full moon. Hereditary
lycanthropes do not have to shift - they have
control over their shifts. However, there are
certain conditions which can cause them to shift
unwillingly:


- Taking damage from a silver weapon

- Entering the _frightened_ condition

- _Vicious Mockery_ spell

- _Tasha’s Caustic Brew_ spell

- DM’s discretion: once every full moon


If these take place, the lycanthrope must make
a Wisdom saving throw. The DC of the check is
18 - the character level in the werewolf class/2
(rounded down). On a failed save, they
immediately shift into their Hybrid form. On a
successful save, they can choose to resist the
shift, and do not neeed to roll again for 24 hours.
If their moon is full, they have disadvantage on
this saving throw. If their moon is new, (dark
moon) they have advantage on this saving throw.
Finally, being under the effect of the spell _Calm_
_Emotions_ gives advantage.

## THE MOONS


Moon Features represent instinctual,
supernatural advantages granted by the
character’s dominant moon. A character selects
one Moon at level 3. Additional Moon Features
are gained at levels 6, 10, 14, and 18.


Unless stated otherwise, Moon Features apply
in Shifter, Hybrid, Dire, and Animal forms.
